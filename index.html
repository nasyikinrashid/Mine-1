🎮 Mission Guide

Read the challenge and select a glowing data target. Use your mouse or touch, or press keys 1–4 to choose an answer.

Correct answers restore the archive and earn 10 knowledge points on the first try. If your first answer is incorrect, read the explanation and try again. You can still progress, but that question won't earn first-attempt points.

There is no time limit. After each correct answer, press Continue to proceed. At the end, review all 15 explanations and your first-attempt accuracy.
